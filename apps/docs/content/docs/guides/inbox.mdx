title: Inbox and notifications
description: Read comments, reply to conversations, and choose the alerts you receive.

Use **Inbox** to read and answer activity from connected accounts. Comments, direct messages, and notifications have separate controls. Turn on only the account features you need; a provider may support one without supporting another.

## Turn on comments or messages

Open **Settings → Workspace → Social accounts** and select an account. Inspect **Comments and replies** and **Direct messages**, then enable each supported feature separately. If an option is unavailable, read its explanation. You may need another provider permission or a supported account type.

Turning a feature off stops its future activity. Saved history stays available and the social account remains connected.

## Read and reply to comments

Open **Inbox → Comments**. Filter by account, unread state, or archived activity, then select a comment. Its detail shows the original post and the known parent replies. Check both the selected person and the receiving account above the public reply field before sending. A public reply is visible on the social network.

OpenPost queues the action and keeps a receipt so retrying an interrupted request does not create another reply. If the provider rejects it, inspect the reported failure before retrying. Marking a comment read does not mean you have replied or resolved it.

Moderation actions depend on the provider. Use the actions shown for that comment to hide, delete, or respond. Use **Load older** to reach saved history beyond the first page.

## Work with direct messages

Open **Inbox → Chats** to browse existing conversations, ordered by recent activity. Search people and saved messages, filter by receiving account, or choose **Unread**. Each conversation identifies the network and the account you will reply from. Starting a new conversation is not available.

Select a conversation for its full-screen chat. OpenPost keeps your draft for that conversation on the device. New messages do not pull you away while you read older history. Use **Load older messages** for earlier saved messages. Where supported, **Load older history from network** queues another history page.

**Archive** removes a chat from the main list. Use **Undo** immediately or find it under **Archived**. A new incoming message returns the chat to the main list.

While Inbox is open, OpenPost collects and displays updates according to the provider's supported transport and limits. Leaving the app stops its foreground connection. Reopening Inbox refreshes stored history; mobile push alerts are not part of this release.

Check the conversation's provider restrictions before replying:

- Facebook and Instagram enforce a customer-service reply window. OpenPost blocks sends after the stored deadline.
- X requires direct-message permission for the app and connected account. **Manage X messaging** opens account settings for separate messaging authorization when needed. Publishing authorization stays connected. Event subscriptions require administrator configuration and an explicit budget; ordinary authorization does not activate them. Encrypted XChat conversations are unsupported.
- Bluesky chat requires an app-password session.
- Mastodon direct posts are visible to the mentioned accounts and involved servers. They are not end-to-end encrypted.

The send state describes what OpenPost knows:

- **Sending…** means the app is submitting your request.
- **Queued** means OpenPost saved it for delivery.
- **Sent** means the provider accepted the message. It does not establish delivery or a read receipt.
- **Delivery unconfirmed** means the request may have reached the server or provider. Keep the message and use its safe recovery action. OpenPost reuses the original request identity and blocks a resend when the provider outcome remains ambiguous.
- **Failed** retains the message and its error. Reconnect an expired account or fix the reported issue before retrying.

Sending a direct message does not create a post. Read indicators appear only when the provider supplies that state.

## Choose email notifications

Open **Settings → Personal → Notifications**. For each optional event, choose **Off**, **Immediate**, or **Daily email**. Set a delivery time and timezone for the daily digest. If email delivery is unavailable, a self-hosted administrator must configure an [email provider](/self-hosting/email).

In-app notifications still appear immediately. Security, access, invitation, and critical billing messages use their own delivery rules.

Use a temporary **Mute** to pause optional email for every workspace or one selected workspace until a chosen time. **End now** restores your saved choices early. Muting does not disable in-app alerts or transactional messages.

Notifications for a post link to the affected result. If only one destination failed, inspect that variant and retry it without reposting to the destinations that succeeded.
